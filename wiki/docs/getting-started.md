# Getting started

**NEOTOKYO Rebuild** (NT;RE) is a tactical FPS with three [[classes|classes]] with unique [[vision-modes|vision modes]] and [[thermoptic-camouflage|thermoptic cloaking]]. The primary game mode, [[capture-the-ghost|Capture the Ghost]], is a modification of classic capture the flag gameplay.

NT;RE plays differently from most shooters. These are the rules that decide fights. Each one links to the full detail.

## 1. You get one life per round

In [[capture-the-ghost|Capture the Ghost]], the main mode, you don't respawn until the next round. A round is about 3 minutes of play, after a 15-second freeze.

A round ends when:

- someone captures the ghost
- one team is wiped out
- time runs out. That's a draw, whichever team holds the ghost.

## 2. Headshots aren't special

Aim at centre mass. A hit to the head does no more damage than a hit to the chest:

- head and chest: ×1.0
- stomach and arms: ×0.75
- legs: ×0.6

There's no damage falloff over distance. Every bullet does ×1.485 its listed base damage. See [[damage|Damage & penetration]].

## 3. Your class decides how you fight

| Class | Play it as | Key trait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[recon\|Recon]] | Scout, flanker | Free sprint, {{stat:player:classes.recon.stats.cloak_capacity}} s cloak, super jump; only {{stat:player:classes.recon.stats.max_health}} HP |
| [[assault\|Assault]] | Rifleman | Motion vision spots moving cloakers; widest gun choice |
| [[support\|Support]] | Anchor, suppression | {{stat:player:classes.support.stats.max_health}} HP, thermal sees through smoke; can't sprint or cloak |

## 4. XP unlocks weapons

XP raises your rank, and each rank unlocks better primaries.

- A kill, an assist or a round win earns 1 XP.
- A teamkill or a suicide costs 1 XP.
- A **ghost capture** moves every surviving teammate straight up to the next rank.

A capture is worth far more than a few kills. XP resets at the start of every match. See [[loadouts|Loadouts & ranks]].

## 5. Watch what's in your hands

The weapon you're holding sets how fast you move. It ranges from ×1.0 down to ×0.675 for the [[pz252|PZ252]].

To rotate faster, switch to your sidearm or knife (×0.85). See [[movement|Movement]].

## 6. The cloak isn't invisibility

[[thermoptic-camouflage|Thermoptic camouflage]], the cloak, is hard to see while you stand still. But Assault's motion vision highlights you the moment you move.

Switching the cloak on makes you flash, and so does firing an unsuppressed weapon. Suppressed weapons keep you hidden.

## 7. Don't shoot teammates early

Friendly fire is on. For the first 7 seconds of each round, damage you do to teammates is reflected back to you at double strength. See [[damage#team-damage|Team damage]].
