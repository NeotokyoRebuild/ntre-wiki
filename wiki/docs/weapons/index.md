# Weapons

NT;RE has {{stat:weapon-count}} weapons and items. Your [[classes|class]] and [[loadouts|rank]] decide which **primary** you can pick.

Each class always spawns with its own **sidearm**, a knife, and a grenade or detpack.

!!! info "Two things to know"
    Every bullet's damage is multiplied by **1.485**, and the numbers below already include it.

    There is **no headshot bonus**: head and chest both take ×1.0. See [[damage|Damage & penetration]].

## Suppressed weapons

Suppressed variants (marked <span class="tag">S</span>) have no muzzle flash. Firing one doesn't make your [[thermoptic-camouflage|cloak]] flash either.

In exchange, they usually have less damage, penetration or rate of fire than the unsuppressed version. For example:

- the [[srm-s|SRM-S]] fires at 660 RPM, against the [[srm|SRM]]'s 990
- the [[mx-s|MX-S]] has penetration 18, against the [[mx|MX]]'s 38

## For server admins

The 1.485 damage multiplier is the server setting `sv_neo_wep_dmg_modifier`.
